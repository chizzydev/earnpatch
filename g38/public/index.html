Refund conditions & delivery trust

Refunds become available if the merchant cancels, the target is not reached by the funding deadline, or the delivery deadline passes without your acceptance. The contract checks the exact published EarnPatch version and artifact. That check is not a quality verdict: your approval is still required. Merchant/developer collusion cannot release your grant without your signature.
